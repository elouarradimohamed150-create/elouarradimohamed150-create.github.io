IPTV in Norway, Israel, Romania and Chile: Four Specialist Services

Updated 8 November 2026

Viewers in smaller markets often get the least help when choosing a TV service. Each of these four services focuses on one country and its language; this page summarises what they publish.

Norway

IPTV Norwegian

IPTV Norwegian — Best for Norway. A single month costs NOK 222, and the longest plan brings it down to about NOK 0.08/month on the 24 months plan. Screens: Choose devices at checkout. Trial: Free 1-day trial. Refunds: 14-day right of withdrawal; if you start at once you pay only for the days used.

  • 14-day right of withdrawal under Norwegian law
  • Free one-day trial
  • No auto-renewal
  • Keep in mind: Prices in NOK only

Israel

Israel IPTV

Israel IPTV — Best for Israel. A single month costs ₪55, and the longest plan brings it down to about ₪15.21/month on the 24 months plan. Screens: Choose devices on WhatsApp. Trial: ₪13 one-day plan. Refunds: Full refund within 7 days for new subscriptions, unless more than 10 hours were watched.

  • Full refund within 7 days
  • ₪13 day plan to test
  • 24 months for ₪365
  • Keep in mind: No refund after 10 hours of viewing

Romania

Romanian IPTV

Romanian IPTV — Best for Romania. There is no one-month plan; the best value works out at about €5.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: Choose devices at checkout. Trial: Free test. Refunds: Money-back guarantee for new customers; the policy page gives no time limit.

  • Free server switch
  • Free app activation
  • Keep in mind: No one-month plan

Chile

Chile IPTV

Chile IPTV — Best for Chile. There is no one-month plan; the best value works out at about CLP 3.46/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: 1 to 3 devices (2 and 3-device plans priced in euros). Trial: Free test. Refunds: 7-day guarantee on the plan cards.

  • Prices in Chilean pesos for one device
  • Free server switch
  • Keep in mind: Multi-device prices are shown in euros

Checks for smaller markets

  • Prices in a single, clear currency.
